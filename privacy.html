Privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

docnearme is a private, login-protected map of Australian GPs and specialists. This page explains what information it holds, where that information comes from, how it is used, and how to have it corrected or removed.

In short: docnearme holds professional directory details about practitioners and practices. It does not hold patient information, and it does not use advertising or analytics trackers.

Who this policy covers

  • Practitioners and practices whose professional details appear on the map.
  • Authorised users who sign in to use the map.

Information about practitioners and practices

For each listed practitioner docnearme may hold:

  • name, specialty and sub-specialty or areas of interest;
  • practice name, practice address, suburb, postcode and state;
  • practice phone number, professional email address and website, where these are published;
  • a link to the public profile the listing came from;
  • whether the practice currently shows bookable appointments on a public booking site, and the next published times;
  • the practice's public Google Maps listing name, star rating and number of reviews;
  • for some GPs: published qualifications and fellowship year, hospital affiliations, other practice locations and a public professional-network profile link;
  • an internal priority label ("tier"), with the score and career-stage notes used to set it.

docnearme does not collect health information, patient information, Medicare or provider numbers, or private (home or personal) contact details.

Where the information comes from

Listings are compiled from sources that are publicly available on the internet: professional directory profiles, hospital and practice "find a doctor" pages, practice websites, public appointment-booking pages and public Google Maps listings. Details are not collected from practitioners directly unless they contact us. The About the data page describes the sources and how often they are refreshed.

How the information is used

  • to understand which practitioners and practices operate in an area and in which specialties;
  • to contact practices in a professional capacity about docnearme services;
  • to keep the listing accurate and remove out-of-date entries.

Listings are not sold. Any message sent to a practice identifies who it is from and can be declined; if a practice asks not to be contacted, it is not contacted again.

Information about people who sign in

  • Sign-in: access needs a username and password. docnearme does not create individual user profiles.
  • Server logs: the hosting provider records standard request logs (such as IP address, time and page requested) to operate and secure the service.
  • Your browser: one preference (light or dark theme) is saved in your browser's local storage. There are no advertising cookies and no analytics or tracking scripts.
  • Approximate location: when the map opens, the server estimates the city for your IP address using the GeoJS lookup service (geojs.io), so the map can start near you. Your IP address is sent to that service for the lookup. Your IP address and the estimated location are kept in server memory for about 24 hours so a reload does not repeat the lookup; they are not written to a database or linked to who you are.
  • Exact location (optional): if you press the locate button, your browser asks your permission and then uses your device location, inside your browser only, to centre the map and sort by distance. It is not sent to docnearme's server and is not stored.
  • Contact form: if you send a message through the contact form, the name, email address and message you enter are sent to us by email through a third-party email delivery service and are used only to deal with your request. The form sets no cookies. Your IP address is used briefly, in server memory only, to limit abuse of the form; it is not included in the message.
  • Third-party content: the map loads its background (style, tiles and fonts) from CARTO (cartocdn.com; map data © OpenStreetMap contributors) and the mapping library from the cdnjs network (Cloudflare). Those two providers receive your IP address when your browser requests their files, as with any website, and CARTO can see which area of the map is being requested.

Storage, security and overseas hosting

The map is hosted with Railway on servers located in the United States, so the information described above is stored outside Australia. Access is restricted to authorised users with a password, and connections are encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No system is perfectly secure, but access is deliberately limited and the data held is restricted to professional directory details.

Accuracy, correction and removal

Because listings come from public sources, details can be out of date. If you are a practitioner or practice manager you can ask at any time to:

  • see the details held about you or your practice;
  • have them corrected; or
  • have your listing removed and not re-added.

Send the request through the contact form, with the practitioner's name and practice so the right entry can be found. Requests are actioned as soon as practicable and in any case within 30 days.

Questions and complaints

For any privacy question or complaint, use the contact form. If you are not satisfied with the response you can contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner at oaic.gov.au.
