About the data

Last updated 19 November 2026

docnearme maps Australian GPs and specialists so it is quick to see who practises where, in which specialty, and how to reach the practice. This page explains what you are looking at.

What is on the map

You seeWhat it means
Coloured dotsOne practitioner each. The colour is the internal priority tier (A to D).
Blue numbered circlesA group of nearby practitioners. Click to zoom in. If they share one location, a list opens instead.
Green ● badge (Today, Tomorrow or a date)The practice currently shows appointments on a public booking page; the next published times are shown.
★ ratingThe practice's public Google Maps rating and review count, matched to the practice (not the individual).
✉ emailA professional email address is published for the practitioner or practice.

Where it comes from

  • Practitioner listings: public professional directory profiles and hospital or practice "find a doctor" pages.
  • Appointment availability: public HotDoc booking pages. GPs: checked about every 30 minutes for around 40 of the busiest GP suburbs in the Sydney region only. Specialists: checked nationally, for every listed specialist found on HotDoc, about every 3 hours. No badge means not checked or not bookable online, not unavailable.
  • Ratings: public Google Maps listings, matched to the practice by name and address.
  • Pharmacies & hospitals: the National Health Services Directory, via the Geoscience Australia Health Facilities dataset — © Commonwealth of Australia (Geoscience Australia), licensed CC BY 4.0; incorporates G-NAF © Geoscape Australia. Additional pharmacies compiled from the Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).
  • Dentists: compiled from the Overture Maps Foundation (CDLA Permissive 2.0), OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), and HotDoc.
  • Map background: © OpenStreetMap contributors, styled by CARTO.

How places are positioned

A pin is at the practice's street address wherever that address is known and could be matched: this covers nearly all GPs and most specialists. Where no street address could be confirmed, the practitioner is shown inside their suburb rather than at their door; their details say "Approximate position". Nobody is scattered at random, so several practitioners can share one pin: click it to open the list of everyone there.

Searching

  • Type any combination of name, practice, suburb, postcode, specialty or phone number, for example cardiologist chatswood.
  • Everyday wording works too (eye doctor, gp near me, heart specialist), and small spelling slips are corrected; when that happens the page says which word it also searched for.
  • Press / to jump to search, ↓ and ↑ to move through results and Enter to open one.
  • "In map view" limits the list to what is visible on the map.
  • The map opens near your approximate area (estimated from your network). Press the locate button on the map to use your exact position and sort by "Nearest".

Accuracy

All details come from third-party public sources and can be out of date. Availability and ratings change constantly. Always confirm with the practice. The tier label is an internal planning aid and is not a measure of clinical quality.

Corrections and removal

If you are a practitioner or practice manager and want your details corrected or removed, use the contact form. See the privacy policy for more.
